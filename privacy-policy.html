Privacy Policy — MeloTube

Effective date: October 18, 2026  •  Last updated: October 18, 2026

MeloTube ("we", "our", "us") is a music discovery and playback app for Android, developed by Zabidev ("Music for your vibe"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using MeloTube, you agree to this policy.

Short version: You can use MeloTube without creating an account. Your playlists, favorites and listening history are stored on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, we store basic profile information to sync your preferences. We show ads to free users (via Google AdMob) and process Premium upgrades through Google Play. We never sell your personal data.

1. Information we collect

a) Information you provide

  • Google account (optional): If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. Signing in is optional — all core music features work without an account. Sign-in is only required before purchasing MeloTube Premium.
  • Support contact: If you contact us, we receive your message and contact details.

b) Information stored on your device

  • Playlists, favorites, listening history, queue, downloads, app settings, theme and appearance choices. This data stays in the app's local database on your device.
  • If you are signed in, a copy of your preferences (e.g. theme, settings) and non-sensitive account metadata may be synced to your private cloud record so your experience is consistent.
  • Downloaded audio files are stored on your device storage. Only tracks the content provider has authorized for download can be downloaded.

c) Information collected automatically

  • Advertising ID: Used by Google AdMob to serve ads. You can reset or limit it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Diagnostics: Anonymous crash and performance information may be collected to fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Consent choices: Your advertising consent selections (e.g. via Google's consent form in regions where it is required).

2. Permissions the app requests (and why)

  • Music / audio files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): To find and play music files already stored on your device (Local Music). Your files are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To show playback controls (play/pause/next) in the notification shade and on the lock screen.
  • Internet: To search and stream music from licensed providers, load artwork, sign in, sync data and serve ads.
  • Advertising ID: To serve ads to free users.

You can deny optional permissions and continue using the rest of the app. The app never asks for contacts, location, camera or microphone access.

3. How we use information

  • To provide music search, streaming, downloads and playback.
  • To remember your playlists, favorites, settings and appearance choices.
  • To manage your account and Premium entitlement (free vs premium features, restores after reinstall).
  • To serve ads to free users and keep the app free (Premium users see zero ads).
  • To obtain legally required advertising consent in regions such as the EEA/UK (via Google's User Messaging Platform).
  • To fix crashes, improve performance and prevent abuse.

4. Music sources & third-party services

MeloTube plays music from the following providers; your search queries and streaming requests go directly to them and are subject to their privacy policies:

  • Jamendo and Audius — free, licensed full-track streaming.
  • iTunes Search API — used for song discovery only (30-second previews and links to external stores). Previews are never presented as full tracks and can never be downloaded.

We also use these third-party services, which process data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (ads) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore (optional sign-in, preference sync) — Firebase Privacy
  • Google Play Billing (Premium purchases) — payments are processed by Google; we never see or store your card details.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) (advertising consent).

5. Advertising & consent

  • Free users see ads; Premium users see zero ads.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob and may be personalized based on your advertising ID and consent choices.
  • In regions where the law requires it (e.g. EEA/UK), the app shows Google's consent form before personalized ads. You can change your choices anytime from the app's Privacy/Ads settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only:

  • With the service providers listed above, as needed to operate the app.
  • If required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.
  • In connection with a merger or acquisition (users would be notified).

7. Data retention & deletion

  • Local app data (playlists, favorites, history, downloads) stays on your device until you clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or delete it in-app.
  • Signing out does not delete your local playlists, favorites or settings.
  • Your cloud record (if you signed in) is tied to your Google account. To request deletion of your account data, use the in-app Account settings or email us at the address below; we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Purchase records are retained as required by Google Play and tax law.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (encrypted connections, authenticated access rules, least-privilege cloud security rules). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children's privacy

MeloTube is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact us at the email below to exercise these rights.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date, and where appropriate, notified in the app.
