Account & Data Deletion

Delete your MeloTube account

You can delete your MeloTube account and its associated cloud data at any time — directly inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app installed. This page explains both options, what gets deleted, and what is kept.

App: MeloTube — Music for your vibe Developer: Zabidev Android package: com.zabidev.melotube

1 Delete inside the app Fastest

  1. Open MeloTube on your Android device
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

In-app deletion is immediate and self-service: your cloud data is removed, your sign-in is revoked, and the app returns to its signed-out state.

2 Request by email No app needed

If you uninstalled the app or can't open it, email us your deletion request. For your security, we can only process requests sent from the email address linked to your MeloTube account — this is how we verify you own the account.

What gets deleted

  • Your MeloTube sign-in (Firebase Authentication account)
  • Your cloud profile: name, email, profile photo reference
  • Your synced preferences (theme, appearance, app settings)
  • Your Premium entitlement metadata held by MeloTube

What is kept or not affected

  • Music files on your device are never deleted by account deletion. To remove local app data, clear the app's storage or uninstall it in Android Settings.
  • Google Play purchase records (subscriptions, order history) are controlled by Google, not by MeloTube — we cannot delete them. Manage or cancel subscriptions at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
  • Support correspondence about your request may be kept as needed to resolve it.

Before you delete — Premium users

Deleting your MeloTube account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. If you have an active Premium subscription, cancel it separately in the Google Play Store (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) to avoid future charges.

How requests are verified & processed

  • Requests must come from the same email address you used to sign in to MeloTube — this verifies account ownership.
  • If we can't match the email to an account, we'll reply asking for verification; we never delete on unverified requests.
  • Verified requests are processed within 30 days; you'll receive a confirmation email when done.
  • In-app deletion takes effect immediately.
