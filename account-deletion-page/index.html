What gets deleted

When your deletion request is processed, we remove:

  • Your MeloTube sign-in (Firebase Authentication account)
  • Your cloud profile: name, email, profile photo reference
  • Your synced preferences (theme, appearance, app settings)
  • Your Premium entitlement metadata held by MeloTube

What is kept or not affected

  • Music files on your device are never deleted by account deletion. To remove local app data, clear the app's storage or uninstall it in Android Settings.
  • Google Play purchase records (subscriptions, order history) are controlled by Google, not by MeloTube — we cannot delete them. Manage or cancel subscriptions at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Deletion-request records

Emails and records about your deletion request are kept for up to 12 months after your request is completed, so we can prove the request was received and properly handled, resolve any disputes, and meet legal record-keeping obligations. They are not used for marketing and are not shared except as required by law.

Before you delete — Premium users

Deleting your MeloTube account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. If you have an active Premium subscription, cancel it separately in the Google Play Store (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) to avoid future charges.

How requests are verified & processed

  • Send your request from the same email address you used to sign in to MeloTube — this starts our ownership-verification process.
  • We may ask for additional confirmation to verify you own the account. Nothing is deleted on an unverified request.
  • Verified requests are processed within 30 days; you'll receive a confirmation email when done.
